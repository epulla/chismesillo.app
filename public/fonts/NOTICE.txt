Fonts bundled with chismesillo
==============================

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/

They are self-hosted rather than loaded from a CDN so that the only network
request the app makes stays the Whisper model weights.

Each file is the "latin" subset (U+0000-00FF), which covers every character the
English and Spanish locales need.

  fraunces.woff2           Fraunces (variable, opsz + wght)
                           Copyright The Fraunces Project Authors
                           https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces

  nunito.woff2             Nunito (variable, wght)
                           Copyright The Nunito Project Authors
                           https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito

  atkinson-mono-400.woff2  Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono
                           Copyright Braille Institute of America, Inc.
                           https://github.com/googlefonts/atkinson-hyperlegible
